### ✍️ 写作训练 (20:40-21:00)

**学习内容：** 日常英文日记 + 基础句型学习

**写作主题（第1天）：**
"My day today" - 描述今天的日常生活

**日记模板（150-200词）：**
```
Date: Monday, September 15, 2024

Today was a busy day. I woke up at 7 am and had breakfast with my family. 
After that, I went to work/school. 

At work, I [做了什么]. It was [形容词]. I learned something new today: [一个新词汇].

In the afternoon, I [下午做了什么].

In the evening, I [晚上做了什么].

Overall, today was [总体评价]. I feel [感受].
```

**第1周学习的5个基础句型：**
```
1. 简单句 - I went to school.
2. 并列句（and）- I woke up and had breakfast.
3. 复合句（because）- I was tired because I studied late.
4. 过去式 - I did my homework yesterday.
5. 现在完成时 - I have learned English for 5 years.
```

**具体任务：**
1. 按模板写 150-200 词日记
2. 在日记中至少用 3 个新词汇
3. 检查语法和拼写
4. 大声读一遍

**第1天日记范例：**
```
Date: Monday

Today was my first day of IELTS preparation. I woke up at 7 am and had breakfast. 
Then I started my study plan. First, I learned 50 new English words about ability, 
access, and achieve. It was challenging but interesting.

In the afternoon, I listened to BBC 6 Minute English. The topic was about staying healthy. 
I didn't understand all of it, but I caught some key phrases.

Then I read an article from News in Levels. It was easy to understand. I learned new words 
like 'habit' and 'routine'.

In the evening, I practiced writing. It was difficult to express my thoughts in English, but I tried my best.

Overall, I feel excited and motivated. I believe I can achieve my goal of Band 7.
```

### 常见问题解答

**Q1: 背单词太慢怎么办？**
A: 第一周不要求全部背会，只需要能认识。重复见面就会记住。

**Q2: 听不懂 BBC 怎么办？**
A: 正常的。看英文字幕，多听几遍。学习 5-10 个新短语就是进步。

**Q3: 读完新闻但过几天就忘记了？**
A: 用艾宾浩斯遗忘曲线复习法。今天学的，明天复习一遍，一周后再复习一遍。

**Q4: 写日记很困难？**
A: 从简单的 5 句话开始。不要求完美，只要能表达意思即可。

**Q5: 发音总是不对怎么办？**
A: 录音对比。用手机录下自己的发音，与原音对比，找出区别。
